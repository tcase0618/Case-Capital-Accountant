Date locked: August 30, 2026
Paper book launch target: Monday, August 31, 2026

## Objective

Validate whether the Lane 1 forensic-quality strategy can outperform the S&P 500 on a net basis before expanding engineering effort into Lane 2 event trades or Lane 3 shorts.

## Fixed Hurdle

Lane 1 passes only if, over the 3-year holdout window, net of 25 basis points round-trip transaction cost and realized turnover:

- CAGR beats SPY total return by at least 300 basis points.
- Maximum drawdown is no worse than SPY drawdown plus 500 basis points.
- The result holds across at least two of three sector-neutral sub-slices.

## Rules

- The evaluation contract is fixed before the holdout run.
- The holdout window is not to be redefined after results are known.
- Integrity gates remain binary.
- Quality gates are sector-relative.
- Valuation gates remain separate from integrity and quality gates.
- Model families must route issuers into operating company, financials, REIT/asset-heavy, pre-revenue, or excluded.
- Lane 1 is limited to operating companies until the other families have dedicated models.
